▸ WHY SPACE IS THE DARK HORSE
  • Launch costs collapsing: Starship targets ~$200/kg to LEO. Falcon 9 was $2,700/kg a decade ago. At $200/kg, entirely new business models become viable — orbital data centers, space manufacturing, pharmaceutical crystallization in microgravity.
  • Rocket Lab (RKLB) is the closest public comp to early SpaceX: They now own launch (Electron + Neutron coming), satellite manufacturing, AND spectrum (Iridium acquisition). Peter Beck is cloning the vertical integration playbook at 1/10th the valuation of where SpaceX would trade.
  • Orbital compute is happening now: StarCloud launches their first orbital data center in January 2026. Philip's thesis from the Moonshots podcast: energy + infrastructure is 30%+ of terrestrial data center cost. In space, cooling is passive, power is solar, and there's no 5-year grid permitting queue.
  • Direct-to-phone satellite: AST SpaceMobile has a working prototype delivering 5G from low Earth orbit. Starlink is 2 years from direct-to-phone video streaming. The telecom industry is about to get disrupted from above.
  • The timing: The space economy in 2026 is where AI was in 2019. The infrastructure is built. The cost curves are bending. The exponential hasn't hit yet — but the slope is about to change.
