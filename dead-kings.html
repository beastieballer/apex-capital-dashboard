Our Story

Born from
the Streets

Dead Kings Co is a creative collective built on raw energy, authentic storytelling, and a relentless pursuit of the perfect shot. We create imagery that moves — editorial, portrait, streetwear, and lifestyle work that lives beyond the feed.

Founded by Harlan Kantner, the brand merges underground culture with high fashion sensibility. Every frame is intentional. Every collaboration is personal.
